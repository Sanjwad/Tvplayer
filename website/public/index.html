ChinookTV is a player. It doesn't include any channels, movies or shows: you'll need your own subscription from an IPTV provider.

Install in three steps

Works on Android TV, Google TV, NVIDIA Shield, Onn and Amazon Fire TV devices.

  1. Get Downloader

    Install the free Downloader app from your TV's app store.

  2. Enter the address

    In Downloader, type chinooktv.com/get and install ChinookTV when it finishes.

  3. Sign in with your provider

    Open ChinookTV and enter the server, username and password (or portal and MAC) your provider gave you.
