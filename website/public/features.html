Everything in one place

Please note: TV Pal is a media player only. It doesn't come with any channels, movies or shows, and it isn't connected to any TV provider. To watch anything, you sign in with a subscription you already have. Paying for TV Pal unlocks the app's features only; it never includes or gives access to any content.

Bring the login your IPTV provider gave you. TV Pal does the rest on your Android TV, NVIDIA Shield, Fire TV Stick or Google TV device.

Live TV with a real guide

Channel groups, favourites and a full programme guide. Missed something? Jump back with catch-up on channels that support it.

Movies and shows that look the part

Posters, IMDb and TMDB ratings, cast pages, trivia and "more like this", even when your provider's own listings are bare.

Your list follows you

My List, favourite channels and where you're up to in every show stay the same on every TV in the house. Kids can keep their own.

Pick up where you left off

Continue watching, the next episode plays on its own, and a long press marks a whole season as watched.

Works with your provider

Xtream Codes logins and Stalker (MAC address) portals, so it works with most providers out of the box.

Keeps playing

When a stream hiccups, TV Pal quietly reconnects and carries on, instead of stopping with an error.

Coming from TiVimate?

You'll feel at home on live TV. Movies and shows are where TV Pal pulls ahead.

What stays the same

Channel groups, favourites, a full guide and catch-up, all driven by the remote the way you're used to. Your Xtream or MAC login works as it is.

What's new

Movie and show pages with ratings, cast and trivia; a list that syncs between TVs; the next episode starting by itself; and a long press to mark a season watched.
